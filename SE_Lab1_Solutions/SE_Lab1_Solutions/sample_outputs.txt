=== 4. Agile Model ===
Key Features:
  - Iterative and incremental development organized into short time-boxed sprints (1-4 weeks).
  - Prioritizes working software, customer collaboration, and responding to change.
Typical Phases:
  1. Concept & Product Backlog Creation
  2. Sprint Planning
  3. Iterative Execution (Design, Code, Test, Daily Standups)
  4. Sprint Review & Retrospective
  5. Continuous Release & Maintenance
Advantages:
  - Highly adaptable to changing requirements even late in development.
  - Continuous delivery of business value and high customer satisfaction.
Disadvantages:
  - Less predictable timelines and budgets if scope evolves continuously.
  - Minimal formal documentation can complicate long-term maintenance or onboarding.
