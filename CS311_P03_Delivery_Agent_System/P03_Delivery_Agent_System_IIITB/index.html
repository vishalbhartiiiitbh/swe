CS311 Software Engineering Design Specification (Section 5 Requirements)

Complete mapping of Actors, Use Cases, Data Model Entities, and Business Constraints implemented in this application.

(a) System Actors

  • Online Shopping Portal (External System): Submits automated delivery booking requests with source, destination, and package metadata.
  • Delivery Dispatch Engine (Automated Actor): Evaluates eligible couriers by locality, capacity, cost, and SLA rating to assign the optimal courier.
  • Operations Administrator / Dispatcher: Monitors live deliveries, triggers rescheduling/cancellations, and audits courier rewards/penalties.
  • Courier Partner Agent: Executes pickups/deliveries, updates shipment milestones, and receives reward/penalty notices.
  • Customer (Recipient): Tracks shipment status and requests date rescheduling or cancellation prior to final delivery.

(b) Use Cases per Actor

  • Portal / Customer: UC-01 Book Delivery, UC-02 Check Delivery Status, UC-03 Reschedule Delivery, UC-04 Cancel Booking.
  • Dispatch Engine: UC-05 Auto-Match & Assign Best Courier, UC-06 Generate Booking/Status/Cancellation Receipts.
  • Operations Admin: UC-07 Calculate Courier Rewards & Penalties, UC-08 Archive Completed Deliveries, UC-09 Query Past Services by Courier.
  • Courier Agent: UC-10 Update Transit Status & Complete Delivery.

(c) Relational Data Model (Entities / Tables)

  • COURIERS: courier_id (PK), name, localities[], base_rate, per_kg_rate, sla_days, rating, max_capacity, active_load, reward_balance, is_available
  • DELIVERIES: tracking_id (PK), order_ref, customer_name, source, destination, address, weight_kg, courier_id (FK), scheduled_date, total_cost, status
  • REWARD_PENALTY_LEDGER: notice_id (PK), courier_id (FK), delivery_id (FK), type, amount, remarks, timestamp
  • DELIVERY_ARCHIVE: archive_id (PK), tracking_id, courier_id (FK), route, final_cost, outcome, closed_at

(d) Enforced System Constraints & Rules

  • Rule 1 (Locality Coverage): A courier can only be assigned if both source and destination exist in their serviceable localities.
  • Rule 2 (Capacity & Availability): Couriers marked unavailable or at active_load >= max_capacity are excluded from assignment.
  • Rule 3 (State Transition Guard): Cancelled or Delivered shipments cannot be rescheduled or cancelled again.
  • Rule 4 (Automatic Resource Release): Cancelling or archiving a shipment decrements the assigned courier's active_load.
  • Rule 5 (Immutable Audit Archive): Every terminal delivery (Delivered or Cancelled) is automatically archived with full courier attribution.
